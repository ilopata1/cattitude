# Purpose: Spoken MAYDAY procedure steps for the emergency module.
# Used by: guide_template_assembly._mayday_steps → build_emergency_module
# Module: emergency / mayday.steps (template assembly, not LLM)
# Variables: {callsign} — vessel radio callsign or vessel name
#            {dsc_location} — where the fixed VHF with the distress button is
# The two DISTRESS lines are alternatives. mayday_steps keeps one: the line
# with {dsc_location} when that place is known, otherwise the other line.
#
Tune VHF to Channel 16 — switch to high power (25W)
If the radio has a red DISTRESS button, lift its cover and hold the button for five seconds, then speak
If the radio has a red DISTRESS button on {dsc_location}, lift its cover and hold the button for five seconds, then speak
Say "MAYDAY MAYDAY MAYDAY"
Say "This is {callsign}, {callsign}, {callsign}"
Say "MAYDAY {callsign}"
State your position — GPS lat/long or bearing and distance from known landmark
State nature of distress — fire, sinking, medical, etc.
State assistance required
State number of persons on board
Release transmit button and listen for response
